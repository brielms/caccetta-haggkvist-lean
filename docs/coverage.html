A tile at degree r and quotient k contains the r orders from kr to kr + r − 1. The CH cutoff q is k at the first order and k + 1 at the others.
Color gives one applicable source: a direct classical theorem first, then a deletion consequence, then the public degree-six manuscript, then the capstone. The selected order lists other applicable sources from this comparison. Green compares with the listed bounds; historical priority is a separate question.
Caccetta–Häggkvist and Hamidoune cover degrees two and three, respectively. Hoàng–Reed cover four and five. Degree one and the reciprocal-pair bound n ≤ 2r are elementary.
Shen covers n ≥ 2r² − 3r + 1. Hladký–Král’–Norin force a triangle in oriented graphs when r ≥ 0.3465n.
